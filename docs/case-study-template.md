# Case Study Template

Every case study follows this structure. Content is identical across personas — emphasis and voice shift per `tone-guide.md`, not the underlying facts.

1. **Overview & Role** — project, company/context, timeframe, what you specifically owned.
2. **The Problem** — business problem and user problem, kept distinct.
3. **Constraints** — team, timeline, stakeholders, technical limits.
4. **Process** — research, discovery, synthesis. The how.
5. **Key Decisions & Tradeoffs** — alternatives considered, why you chose what you chose. Usually the section that differentiates a senior designer from a portfolio.
6. **Obstacles / Pivots** — what broke, what changed.
7. **The Solution** — walkthrough of the actual design, including the visual/craft dimension (system, key screens, brand decisions, before/after) — folded in here rather than split into a separate UI section.
8. **Outcomes** — metrics, qualitative feedback, business impact.
9. **Reflection** — what you'd do differently now.

**Video (beta):** one trailer per case study, ~60–90 sec — hook, single most interesting decision/problem, glimpse of craft, outcome. Sits above the written breakdown. Persona-specific cuts are a fast-follow, not required for beta.
