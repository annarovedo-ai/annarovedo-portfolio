## About Page — Recruiter

Eyebrow: Principal Experience Designer
Headline: I turn complex, undefined problems into directions teams can act on.

20+ years across enterprise UX, AI, search, information architecture, design systems, brand, and digital products.
Selected clients include IBM, LinkedIn, Nike, Bloomberg, State Street, and Ford.

Primary CTA: View résumé

**How I approach the work**
I do my best work when the problem matters but the answer is not obvious yet. I use research, rapid prototyping, testing, and systems thinking to uncover what is really happening, make possibilities tangible, and help teams move forward with confidence.

01 Complexity deserves respect
Expert users do not always need less information. They need the right information, organized around how they actually think and work. My goal is not to make complex products appear simple. It is to make their complexity understandable and useful.

02 Make the idea tangible
Prototypes turn assumptions into something a team can examine, question, and test. I make ideas visible early so we can learn before decisions become expensive or difficult to reverse.

03 Create the conditions for better answers
I do not walk into a room pretending to know everything. I connect perspectives across users, business, design, and technology so the strongest direction can emerge from the evidence.

**What I bring to a team**

1. Senior contribution through the work
I lead by framing problems, setting direction, making prototypes, facilitating decisions, and raising the quality of the final experience. I am most effective as a senior individual contributor with the autonomy to explore and the responsibility to deliver.

2. Make the differences visible
Complex projects rarely struggle because a team lacks ideas. They struggle because different disciplines are solving different versions of the problem. I make those differences visible and create shared artifacts that help teams decide together.

3. The system is visible in every detail
People do not experience a design system, an information architecture, or a product strategy in the abstract. They experience the screen in front of them. I make sure the larger logic survives all the way into the content, interaction, and visual details.

**A multidisciplinary career**

From visual culture to digital products
My career began in graphic design, exhibition design, video, fashion, and advertising in Italy, Barcelona, and the US. That background taught me to see design in cultural context, not only as a functional response to a brief.

Independent and embedded
I have worked independently since 2007, frequently joining agency and client teams for substantial engagements. This has taught me how to enter unfamiliar organizations quickly, build trust across disciplines, and contribute without requiring a long runway.

Enterprise complexity and emerging technology
My recent work has focused on complex enterprise experiences, including generative AI, global search, connected product ecosystems, fintech platforms, and large-scale design systems.

A former IBM manager repeatedly placed me on early ideation work rather than production execution. It was a useful recognition of where I create the most value: exploring ambiguous problems and giving teams something concrete enough to evaluate.
